How to use

  • Drag with one finger to turn the specimen.
  • Pinch to zoom; drag with two fingers to move it.
  • Tap any structure or label to learn about it.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys turn, + and − zoom, N and P step through the dissection, 1, 2 and 3 pick Turn, Scalpel and Lift in Explore.
  • In Explore, dissect as in the hall: draw incisions with the Scalpel, then Lift inside the outline to turn the skin flap back. Lift also picks fat and fascia away as you drag, and removes a muscle, vessel or bone with a click. Double-click anywhere to look closer; Undo is in the strip.

Dissect takes you through the specimen in the order of a dissection manual. Explore lets you switch layers on and off freely. Trace lights up the fibres carried by any root or branch. Lesion cuts a nerve and shows which muscles are paralysed and where feeling is lost. Spotter pins structures and quizzes you the way a practical exam does, returning to the ones you missed.
